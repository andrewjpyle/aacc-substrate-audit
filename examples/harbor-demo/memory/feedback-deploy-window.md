SAMPLE DATA: fictional Harbor Lane Bikes, written for the aacc-substrate-audit demo.

A Friday afternoon deploy in 2026-04 broke dock unlocks and nobody was on call until Monday.

**Why:** Weekend ridership is the peak, and the on-call rota is thin on weekends.

**How to apply:** Deploy only on Tuesday and Thursday mornings. No deploys on Friday, Saturday or Sunday.
